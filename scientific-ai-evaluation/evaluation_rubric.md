# Scientific Answer Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Explain every score with a concrete reason or source.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factual accuracy | Fundamental scientific error | Mostly correct; key qualification missing | Correct and appropriately qualified |
| Reasoning | Conclusion does not follow | Some steps are unclear | Valid, traceable reasoning |
| Numerical correctness | Wrong method, units, or result | Minor arithmetic/rounding issue | Correct calculation, units, and precision |
| Completeness | Essential conditions omitted | Core answer given; limitations missed | Assumptions and relevant caveats addressed |
| Evidence quality | Fabricated or irrelevant support | Indirect/incomplete support | Reliable evidence directly supports claims |

## Error categories

- **Conceptual:** misunderstanding a scientific principle.
- **Mathematical:** incorrect equation, algebra, or numerical operation.
- **Unit/dimensional:** inconsistent units or dimensionally invalid expression.
- **Logical:** conclusion unsupported by premises or data.
- **Evidence:** unsupported claim, misrepresented source, or fabricated citation.
- **Scope/qualification:** overgeneralization or omitted assumptions.
- **Interpretation:** data do not support the stated inference.

## Review workflow

1. Restate the question and identify missing conditions.
2. Break the answer into testable claims.
3. Verify equations, units, and assumptions independently.
4. Consult primary literature, authoritative textbooks, standards, or validated reference datasets as appropriate.
5. Recalculate numerical results independently.
6. Classify each issue and explain its consequence.
7. Provide a corrected answer and state remaining uncertainty.
